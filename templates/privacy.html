Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. As you use the app, we also store the expense data you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

We do not collect more information than is necessary to provide the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to operate the core features of Spendly — logging expenses, generating spending summaries, and keeping your account secure. We may also use aggregated, anonymized data to improve the service.

We do not use your personal expense data for advertising, and we do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and is only accessible to you through your authenticated account. We retain your data for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, your data is permanently removed within a reasonable period.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party providers for functions such as hosting and email delivery. These providers only receive the minimum data necessary to perform their function and are not permitted to use it for any other purpose.

We do not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, you can reach out to us at support@spendly.app.
